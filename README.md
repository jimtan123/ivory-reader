# Ivory Reader

A single-file piano trainer for beginners. Open `index.html` in a browser, or use the GitHub Pages site for this repo.

- **Read notes**: staff flashcards answered on an on-screen keyboard, by computer key, or on a MIDI piano.
- **Hand position**: C, Middle C and G positions with finger numbers, posture checklist, finger drill.
- **Practice**: pieces written in a short text notation, rendered on a grand staff, with Listen and Wait modes.
- **Metronome** and **MIDI** in the bottom bar.

MIDI needs Chrome or Edge on a laptop or Android tablet; Safari and iPad have no Web MIDI. Progress and saved pieces stay in the browser you use.

## Moving a piece between copies

This static site and the claude.ai version of the app are two separate copies with separate storage — a piece saved in one does not appear in the other.

There is also a `claude.ai` copy of this same app that can transcribe a photo of sheet music, because it runs inside Claude's own page viewer, something a plain static site like this one cannot do. To bring a scanned piece here:

1. On the claude.ai copy, scan the photo, then press **Copy notation** next to the notation box.
2. Open this site, open **Add a piece**, paste into the Notation box (or press **Paste**), then press **Load and save**.

The same Copy/Paste buttons work in reverse, or between any two copies of the app.
